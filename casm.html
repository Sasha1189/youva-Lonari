Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2026 | Last Updated: May 23, 2026

Lonari Yuva Connect maintains a strict zero-tolerance stance toward any content, activity, behavior, or communication that involves or promotes the sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment of children. This policy applies strictly to all users, creators, and interactions within the Lonari Yuva Connect mobile application.

1. Prohibited Activities & Content

Users are explicitly forbidden from utilizing Lonari Yuva Connect to upload, share, distribute, transmit, or store any materials related to:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): This includes any visual depiction, photos, videos, or computer-generated imagery of minors engaging in sexually explicit conduct.
  • Grooming Behaviors: Any solicitation or communication aimed at exploiting, harassing, or establishing inappropriate contact with children.
  • Extortion & Trafficking: Any predatory behavior or blackmail targeting minors.

2. Content Moderation & Enforcement

All user-generated content and profiles are subject to manual and automated moderation workflows. Upon acquiring any actual knowledge of violations, Lonari Yuva Connect will execute immediate enforcement measures, including:

  • Permanent ban and immediate deletion of offending user accounts.
  • Immediate removal of the illegal content from the platform.
  • Reporting confirmed instances of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional law enforcement agencies (such as the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at https://cybercrime.gov.in).

3. In-App Feedback & Reporting Mechanism

Lonari Yuva Connect provides built-in reporting features directly within the application interface. Users can easily flag problematic content, profiles, or chat interactions for rapid intervention by our security moderation teams.
