Account & Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: May 23, 2026

At Lonari Yuva Connect, we value your privacy and give you full control over your personal information. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

What Data is Deleted?

When you submit a data deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our active databases within 30 days:

  • Your user profile information (Name, Email, Phone number, Profile picture).
  • Your match preferences and connections.
  • Your message history and chat logs.

Data Retention Note

Please note that certain transaction logs or safety records may be temporarily retained where required by law or to ensure platform safety (e.g., if an account was banned for policy violations).

How to Request Deletion

Click the button below to send an email request directly to our data privacy team. Please send the email from the address registered with your account, and include your username.

Request Account Deletion
⚠️ Is the button not working?
If clicking the button does not open your email app, please send a manual email to hpk.developer@gmail.com with the subject line "Lonari Yuva Connect - Account Deletion Request". Make sure to include your Account Phonenumber in the message body so we can process your request.
